Researchers from the DFKI-NLP group will attend the [19th International Conference on Natural Language Generation INLG 2026)](https://2026.inlgmeeting.org/) to present ongoing work from the [GenKI4Media project](https://genki4media.de/) during the poster session. INLG 2026 will take place October 17-21 in Utrecht, the Netherlands. One poster will present a deployed, real-world evaluation of an AI-assisted tool for length-constrained news summarization in a professional editorial workflow. The other post introduces an LLM-based approach for assessing violated press code provisions, violation severity, and generating justifications on test data derived from German Press Council decisions.
