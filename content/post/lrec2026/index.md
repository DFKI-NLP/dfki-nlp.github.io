One paper from DFKI-NLP authors has been accepted for publication at [LREC 2026](https://lrec2026.info/), the 2026  Language Resources and Evaluation Conference. The conference is will take place in Palma de Mallorca, Spain, from May 11th to May 16th, 2026. The paper presents a biomedical relation extraction dataset that has also been annotated for epistemic commitment (factuality) of the relations. On this dataset, we evaluate LLM-based methods for the joint detection of relations and epistemic commitment.
